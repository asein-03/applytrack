Getting started

A few steps.
Then start applying.

You can add applications manually and explore the demo before enabling any tracking.

  1. 1

    Create your account.

    Open the desktop app and choose Create an account. Finish in your browser, confirm your email in that same browser, then return to the app and sign in.

  2. 2

    Pair your browser.

    In Connections, download the extension and follow the installation steps. Paste the pairing code into the extension, then allow the supported job sites when you start tracking.

  3. 3

    Choose what to connect.

    Screen confirmation is optional and checks only the surface you select. Gmail is optional too: sign in to the same ApplyTrack account in your browser, then approve read-only Gmail access.

Your controls

Tracking starts with you.

Stop screen and browser tracking from the app. Disconnect Gmail whenever you choose. Delete saved records or your account in the app.

Gmail checks continue in the cloud while the app is closed. Read-only access cannot send, edit or delete your Gmail messages.
