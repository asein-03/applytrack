This policy explains how ApplyTrack handles information when you use its desktop app, browser extension, cloud workspace and Gmail connection.

What ApplyTrack stores

Account information includes your email address and authentication records. Supabase provides authentication. ApplyTrack does not store your password in its application database.

Your workspace stores the applications you add or capture: employers, roles, job links, application stages, dates, notes, tasks, contact details, document information and supporting evidence. Résumé and cover letter copies captured from supported application forms are stored in private cloud storage when a copy is available.

The app keeps account session credentials on your computer so you can stay signed in. The desktop app uses operating-system protected storage for these credentials. The browser version uses browser storage.

Browser and screen tracking

Browser tracking starts when you enable it. On supported application pages, the extension reads job and submission details and detects selected résumé or cover letter files. You grant access to supported sites when starting tracking.

Screen confirmation is separate and optional. You select a screen, window or tab. Text recognition runs on your computer. ApplyTrack does not save a continuous video recording. When it matches a submission confirmation to an application, it saves a snapshot of the selected surface and the matching confirmation text to your workspace. A snapshot can include other information visible on that surface.

Stop tracking in the app to end capture. Quitting the desktop app also stops device tracking. Closing its window may leave it running in the menu bar.

Gmail access and use

Gmail is optional. When you connect an inbox, ApplyTrack requests the gmail.readonly permission. This permits reading messages and mailbox information; it does not permit sending, changing or deleting your messages.

ApplyTrack reads your connected inbox address, message identifiers, sender and recipient information, subjects, dates and message text to identify application confirmations, assessments, interviews, offers and rejections. The initial check searches the previous 30 days for application-related terms. Later checks examine new messages to find updates. Gmail permission is broader than a particular job or sender.

Clear matches can update existing applications and create next steps. Messages requiring a match or confirmation remain available for your review. Checks run in the cloud even when the desktop app is closed or you sign out.

Raw message data used to verify sender signatures is processed temporarily and is not saved as a raw mailbox archive. Relevant email content is retained: review items can contain up to 50,000 characters of message text, and application evidence can contain excerpts up to 8,000 characters. Message identifiers, signature information and processing results help prevent duplicate updates. Gmail attachments are not added to your document library.

Where information is processed

Supabase hosts account authentication, the private workspace database, document storage and cloud email processing. Google provides Gmail access and authorization. GitHub hosts this public website and app downloads. Those providers process connection and service information under their own privacy policies: Supabase, Google and GitHub.

Cloud communication uses HTTPS. Account access rules restrict workspace data and documents to the signed-in owner. Gmail access and refresh tokens are encrypted separately and kept in service-only storage. This is access-controlled cloud storage, not end-to-end encryption.

Limited use of Google data

ApplyTrack uses Google data to provide your application tracking and email update features. It does not sell your information, use it for advertisements, or use it to create, train or improve AI or machine learning models. The app does not send your email content to an AI model provider.

ApplyTrack’s handling of Google information follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and the Google Workspace API User Data and Developer Policy, including their Limited Use requirements.

Human access to Google data is limited to specific information you explicitly authorize for support, necessary security investigations, or legal obligations. The public website uses no advertising or analytics scripts.

Disconnecting and deleting data

In Connections, disconnect Gmail to stop future inbox checks. ApplyTrack requests removal of its Google access. You can also remove ApplyTrack from your Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not erase application records or email updates already saved in your workspace.

You can delete applications and saved document copies in the app, and dismiss messages awaiting review. Processing identifiers may remain to prevent a deleted application’s emails from being imported again.

To delete your account and its stored workspace data, open Connections → Delete account. Enter your current password and type DELETE. The app confirms completion only after your account, active workspace and stored documents have been removed. If it reports that deletion did not finish, retry to complete it.

Workspace records and saved email text remain until you remove them or delete your account. There is no automatic expiry for those records. If Google cannot complete token revocation immediately, encrypted tokens and minimal cleanup identifiers remain in a restricted retry queue until access removal succeeds. This cleanup queue can outlive account deletion and cannot be used to continue inbox checks.

Help and changes

Use GitHub issues for general product support. Issues are public: never post private messages, documents, account credentials or personal data there. Account and workspace deletion is available inside the app, without posting a public request.

Material changes to these practices will be reflected on this page. The month at the top identifies this policy version.
